# First Ten Contributors — Pilot Campaign

**Objective:** Ten distinct people complete one useful first contribution. This is a learning pilot, not a vanity signup drive.

## What we are recruiting for now
The pilot is limited to low-risk feedback and review:
- Continuity findings on the Film 1 beat sheet.
- Character voice observations.
- Alternate joke suggestions.
- Production benchmark critique.
- Shot-list and sound-design feedback.
- Clarity and onboarding feedback.

Until the rights decision is resolved, do not solicit finished scripts, artwork, music, voice performances or substantial production assets.

## Audience segments and asks

| Audience | Relevant ask | Best first artifact | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screenwriters / comedy writers | Does story and joke logic work? | Beat-sheet continuity review or alternate beat | Asking for a full screenplay |
| Actors / performers | Are characters playable and distinct? | Voice guide or performance note | Unpaid recorded performance |
| Filmmakers / editors | Is the scene producible and cuttable? | Shot list or benchmark critique | Vague “help make a film” pitch |
| AI video creators | What breaks in repeatability and identity? | Benchmark critique | Claiming the pipeline is proven |
| Developers / agent builders | Is task discovery and validation sound? | Manifest/schema critique | Broad repository write access |
| General comedy fans | Which premise/beat is unclear or funny? | One-beat feedback | Technical setup |

## Pilot sequence

### Wave 0 — Readiness
- Verify every public link from a logged-out browser.
- Confirm the rights disclaimer appears in quick-start and outreach copy.
- Ensure the first task takes under 30 minutes.
- Confirm who reviews submissions and when.
- Keep no more than five tasks actively claimed at once.

### Wave 1 — Warm invitations
Invite 5–10 relevant people individually. Ask for one specific review. No mass DMs or scraped contacts.

### Wave 2 — Community participation
Choose at most two communities initially. Read current rules, inspect recent successful posts, and ask moderators when promotion is ambiguous. Contribute to existing conversations before asking for help.

### Wave 3 — Public project update
Publish one tangible update: Film 1 beat sheet exists, the benchmark is specified, and the project is testing a phone-friendly contribution process. Link one starter issue, not the whole repository tree.

### Wave 4 — Review and report
Acknowledge within 72 hours where practical. Review within seven days. Publish a short report of accepted feedback, process changes and next tasks. Do not expose contributor details without permission.

## Pilot success measures
- 10 unique first-time contributors.
- At least 6 usable submissions.
- At least 5 accepted or converted into actionable revisions.
- Every contributor receives acknowledgement.
- Median first response under 72 hours.
- At least 2 repeat contributors within 30 days.

Treat these as pilot targets, not forecasts. If response capacity fails, pause outreach and fix review operations.

## Outreach tracking
Record only channel/community, date, post or invitation URL, audience segment, linked task, responses, submissions, accepted contributions, moderator feedback and next action. Do not collect personal contact details without purpose and consent.
