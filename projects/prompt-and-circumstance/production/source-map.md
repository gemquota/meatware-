## Fidelity policy

- The Markdown set is a structured editorial replacement, not a verbatim transcript.
- Story facts and decisions are derived from the PDF.
- New organization, acceptance criteria, and continuity flags are editorial structuring.
- No external research has been used to “correct” fictional legal, financial, technical, or production claims.
- The original PDF remains the archival source for exact wording and chronology.
- Where the source evolves or contradicts itself, the discrepancy is preserved in the continuity register.

## Source-derived vs. editorial additions

The franchise premise, characters, film titles, sequence concepts, ending, contributor tiers, phase names, and original proposed paths are source-derived.

The continuity IDs, acceptance criteria, governance cautions, and documentation structure are editorial additions intended to make the material usable as a repository.
