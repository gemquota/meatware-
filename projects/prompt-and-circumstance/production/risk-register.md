# Production and Collaboration Risk Register

**Status:** Initial planning register; review as the project develops.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Trigger / owner |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| R01 | Story rights and contributor permissions unclear | High | Critical | Resolve rights model before substantial creative recruitment | Before scripts/assets accepted / maintainer |
| R02 | Recruitment exceeds review capacity | Medium | High | First-ten pilot; cap active claims | Response target missed / maintainer |
| R03 | Canon drift across six outlines | High | High | Canon source, continuity register, editorial gate | Contradictory draft / editor |
| R04 | AI-generated volume mistaken for progress | High | High | Acceptance criteria and human review | High submissions, low accepted output / task owner |
| R05 | Scope expands before Film 1 proves feasibility | High | High | Stage gates and pilot before feature assembly | Work bypasses stage exit / maintainer |
| R06 | Production modality fails continuity/performance gates | Medium | High | Paired benchmark and hybrid fallback | Critical benchmark score below threshold / production lead |
| R07 | Cost estimates omit human cleanup | High | High | Track labour and revision time | Cost per approved minute diverges / production lead |
| R08 | Third-party/generated assets lack usage rights | Medium | Critical | Asset register and clearance review | Asset enters production without provenance / production lead |
| R09 | Contributors feel ignored or exploited | Medium | High | Response targets, small tasks, rights clarity, attribution | Unanswered submissions / maintainer |
| R10 | Public recruitment perceived as spam | Medium | Medium | Tailor posts, follow rules, ask moderators | Removals or negative feedback / outreach owner |
| R11 | Source contradictions become accidental canon | Medium | High | Source map and continuity register | Proposal copied into bible without decision / editor |
| R12 | Personal data or credentials enter public issues | Low | High | Data-minimization notice and prompt moderation | Sensitive information submitted / maintainer |
| R13 | Voice, likeness or music rights overlooked | Medium | Critical | Separate releases and asset-class clearance | Performance or music planned / production lead |

## Review cadence
Review monthly during active development and before each major production gate. Update likelihood, impact, mitigation owner and residual risk when evidence changes.
