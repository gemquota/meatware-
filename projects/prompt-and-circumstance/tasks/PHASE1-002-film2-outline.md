## Context to load

- The Order is a centuries-old analog cabal using paper ledgers, pneumatic tubes, carrier pigeons, physical deeds, and manual accounting.

## Structural problem to solve
The current source treatment has unresolved placement for the false boss, midpoint, and climax. Propose a coherent 40-beat structure that:
1. Establishes the bros' new oligarch status before stripping away their digital advantages.
2. Gives the Order a comprehensible analog operating principle without making it magically omniscient.
3. Places a satisfying apparent antagonist resolution around 33–38% runtime.
4. Uses the midpoint to reveal the larger scope/history of the Order and the network's blind spot.
5. Builds toward the Alpine infiltration, accounting discrepancy, junior-heir mutiny, and mainframe bridge.
6. Preserves the source's paranoid 1970s conspiracy-thriller tone played for slapstick.
