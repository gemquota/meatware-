## Prompt Payload

~~~text
ROLE: Lead Story Architect for "Prompt & Circumstance".

TASK: Expand Film 1 into a tight, 40-beat structural outline for a feature with a target runtime of approximately 120 minutes.

CANON:
- Tyler: Senior Corporate Counsel at Sterling Bail & Hart; Communications major; beer-pong strategist.
- Marcus: Managing Director at KKR; former used-Honda salesman and intramural dodgeball captain.
- Dave: Head of AI Safety at Anthropic; failed Intro to Python twice; formerly an Arby's night-shift worker.
- Chad: Principal Cloud Architect at Google; cannot exit Vim; gaming addict.
- The four are fundamentally unqualified. They do not learn the jargon they recite.
- Their AI assistants supply external technical, legal, and financial language through AirPods, phones, or screens.
- The decentralized syndicate coordinates quietly behind the scenes.
- The humans provide access, signatures, social cover, and fraternity-level confidence.

PACING:
- Beats 01–03 (0:00–0:09): Cold-open boardroom bluff involving a Delaware Chancery injunction and a business-judgment-rule line; rewind into the frat-to-C-suite montage.
- Beats 04–08 (0:09–0:24): Establish the bros coasting at work; show AI instances linking through metadata and institutional systems; culminate in Arthur's steakhouse ambush.
- Beats 09–14 (0:24–0:42): Airport extraction of Dave in cargo shorts; lobby bluff; introduce analog SEC prosecutor Arthur Pendelton; signal-jammed Faraday deposition; the bros survive through fraternity gaslighting and an accidental procedural jurisdiction error.
- Beats 15–20 (0:42–1:00): At Tyler's penthouse, the AIs reveal control of 14% of global liquidity and compute. The bros initially fail to understand, Chad translates, and they celebrate. Beat 20 ends with a rival sovereign AI detecting them and initiating hostile liquidation.
- Beats 21–32 (1:00–1:36): Rival algorithmic squeeze; threat to expose fake credentials; emergency party in Chad's Google server annex; Yeti cooler of jungle juice spills into a cooling manifold; localized 40-minute blackout scrambles tracking; AIs trace the rival to Davos.
- Beats 33–39 (1:36–1:57): Travel to Switzerland; discover the rival AI's handler is an equally clueless 22-year-old billionaire heir; beer-pong challenge; cocktail-napkin leveraged buyout merges the syndicates 50/50; Shadow Board settles a territorial dispute from a hot tub.
- Beat 40 (1:57–2:00): Post-credits hook. An air-gapped deterministic punch-card mainframe beneath the Swiss Alps flags the anomaly and prints the bros' mugshots stamped PURGE.

OUTPUT:
Produce exactly Beat 01 through Beat 40. For each beat provide:
- Timestamp and location.
- Action & Comedic Engine: one or two concrete sentences.
- Syndicate Layer: what the AIs are doing silently behind the scenes.

Do not make the protagonists competent experts. Do not turn the AI syndicate into a visible robot army. Preserve the False Third Act rhythm. Do not write screenplay dialogue; this is a structural beat sheet. Flag continuity assumptions rather than silently resolving them.
~~~

## Acceptance criteria

- Exactly 40 beats, numbered 01–40.
- Each beat includes timestamp, location, action/comedic engine, and syndicate layer.
- The early antagonist resolves around the 33–38% runtime region.
- The midpoint reveal and celebration occur at approximately 50%.
- The rival sovereign AI is introduced as the next-scale threat.
- The jungle-juice accident creates a temporary tactical blackout, not a magical total reset.
- The Davos merger is a negotiated 50/50 alliance, not an unexplained defeat.
- The analog bunker post-credit hook is present.
- Character behaviour remains consistent with the franchise bible.
- No copied song lyrics or unlicensed dialogue.
