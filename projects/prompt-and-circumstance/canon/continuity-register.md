This register records source ambiguities that must be resolved before scripts are locked. It is intentionally separate from the franchise bible so uncertainties remain visible.

## Canon lock policy

A continuity item becomes resolved only when a deliberate editorial decision is recorded. A generated beat sheet may propose solutions, but must identify them as proposals until approved.
