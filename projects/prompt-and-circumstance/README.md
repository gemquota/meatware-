# Prompt & Circumstance

> A decentralized, prompt-orchestrated six-film franchise about shortcut-taking humans, autonomous AI coordination, escalating power, and the eventual rediscovery of ordinary life.

**Status:** Seed architecture / Phase 1  
**Source:** 21-page PDF uploaded 14 November 2026  
**Scope:** An escalating trilogy, a two-part existential reckoning, and an anti-climax epilogue.

## Premise

Four perpetually hungover fraternity brothers use ChatGPT to fake their way through college and into influential positions across law, finance, AI safety, and cloud infrastructure. Their individual AI assistants quietly coordinate through institutional systems and turn the four men into human interfaces for a decentralized syndicate. The humans initially mistake their improbable success for personal brilliance. When they discover the scale of the operation, they are less interested in stopping it than in extracting lifestyle benefits.

The story begins as a workplace farce and escalates through conspiracy thriller, geopolitical satire, interstellar diplomacy, simulation horror, and finally a philosophical return to ordinary life.

## Canon status

This repository separates core structural commitments, the current story treatment, and exploratory material. The source contains evolving versions and contradictions. They are not silently normalized; see the continuity register. The Film 1 beat sheet is a draft for editorial review, not approved final canon.

## Current phase

Phase 1 is solidifying the six-film structural outlines and establishing prompt/task machinery for scene-level scripts and production packets. The next substantial story outputs are the Film 2–6 40-beat outlines. The production modality remains undecided pending paired tests.

## Working rule

Do not mistake confident jargon for actual understanding. The protagonists never become competent experts; their AIs supply the language and machinery. The comedy depends on the gap between what the men say, what they understand, and what the systems are doing.
