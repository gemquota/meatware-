Computational laboratory · Research programme

What happens when nothing is told what life is?

Meatware is an abstract experimental substrate for investigating how evolution, cumulative adaptation and complex organisation might emerge from minimal interacting systems, without encoding biological categories into the machinery.

Explore the repository ↗Read the specification

The substrate makes no promises

Mechanisms first. Interpretations later.

01 / Representation

Entities and relations

Extensible state-bearing entities and recursively composable relations. No fixed organisational hierarchy or privileged biological object types.

02 / Dynamics

General transformations

State changes arise from interactions, internal dynamics and environmental processes. No built-in reproduction or evolution command.

03 / Method

Observation without interference

Analysis measures persistence, variation, propagation, information and adaptation without changing the system it observes.
